about

Hi, I’m Pei, a Mechatronics Engineer specializing in automation, robotics, and precision system design. With a foundation in Mechanical Engineering, I began my career designing medical and biomedical research facilities, where I gained experience managing complex technical requirements and multidisciplinary projects.

later earned a Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering with a focus on robotics and controls, transitioning into advanced manufacturing and automation. My experience includes developing mechatronics systems for EV battery inspection using ultrasound technologies, as well as software-defined manufacturing and assembly equipment.

I am passionate about building intelligent, reliable systems that integrate electromechanical engineering and controls to enable next-generation automation technologies.

During my undergrad years as a machinist, I discovered the joy of creating things from the ground up. That experience sparked a lifelong passion for hands-on learning—whether it’s garment design, sewing, sewing machine repair, furniture making, woodworking, or painting, I’m always finding new ways to create and build.

Please feel free to check out Projects page for engineering work and Art page for hobbies!
